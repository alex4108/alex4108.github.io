Operating principles

Prefer boring reliability

Choose understandable systems, explicit failure modes, and recovery paths someone can run at 2 AM.

Automate with receipts

CI/CD, scripts, and runbooks should produce proof, not merely optimistic logs.

Modernize incrementally

Respect legacy constraints, then carve out safer seams for the next change.

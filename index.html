Operating principles

Make change observable and recoverable

  • Verify resulting state. A successful command is not the same as a correct system.
  • Automate toil. Repetition is a signal to build a safer path.
  • Stage reversible change. Reduce blast radius and keep a tested way back.
  • Treat cost, security, and operability as architecture. They are design inputs, not cleanup work.
